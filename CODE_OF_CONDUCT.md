# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

WrapForge is a working space for designers, installers, developers, and curious
users. We want participation to be free of harassment and hostility, regardless
of experience, identity, background, or opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith and ask before assuming intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be specific and evidence-based. "It crashes" is less useful than steps,
  logs, and versions.
- Respect licensing and attribution. Do not ask others to share assets they
  cannot legally redistribute.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change for scope, safety, or
  maintainability reasons.
- Give credit for ideas and work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory comments about identity, background, or ability.
- Sexualized attention or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, hidden telemetry, or unlicensed
  assets.
- Pressuring users to share confidential project files or credentials.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any
project space. It also applies when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's
private contact channel or GitHub's report/block tools. If a private channel is
not listed, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact method without
including sensitive details.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will avoid
conflicts of interest and will not take part in decisions about reports involving
themselves.

## Enforcement

Responses may include:

1. A private clarification or request to stop.
2. A public correction or warning.
3. Temporary limits on participation.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces.

Severity, repetition, and willingness to repair harm guide the response. Safety
comes before comfort.
